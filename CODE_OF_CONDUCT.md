# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short adaptation; the full text there applies where this one is silent.

## Our pledge

We want participation in WebEdiThor to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we encourage

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Writing in English or Spanish. If you aren't sure someone will understand, a translation helps.

## What we don't accept

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, comments) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([m3nt41](https://github.com/m3nt41)) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled promptly and with respect for the privacy of the reporter.
